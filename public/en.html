−3 · Mobile app security

What an attacker would see, we see first

We audit Android and iOS apps against OWASP’s public standards, with automated tools and manual testing. We also test your AI assistants and agents: prompt injection, data leaks, red teaming. You get a clear report and concrete fixes.

Our security offer
  • MASVSCompliance audit against OWASP’s mobile application security verification standard: storage, cryptography, authentication, network, platform, code, resilience.
  • MASTGTesting method from the OWASP guide, for reproducible, documented checks.
  • MobSFStatic and dynamic analysis of the APK or IPA, followed by manual triage to rule out false positives.
  • PentestMobile penetration test: network traffic, APIs, local storage, bypasses, reverse engineering.

Frequently asked questions

Your questions, our answers

What is sovereign AI?

It is AI you stay in control of: models run on your infrastructure or with the hosting provider of your choice, in Tunisia if needed, and your data is not used to train a third party’s models. You can switch model or provider without rebuilding everything.

Is my data sent to OpenAI, Google or another provider?

Only if you agree. For sensitive data, we favour open models hosted on your side. When an online service is a better fit, we explain clearly what leaves, where it goes, and with what guarantees.

What is an AI agent, and how do you keep it under control?

An AI agent chains several steps of a process: finding information, checking it, preparing an answer or an action. We build it with LangGraph, which defines the path to follow, and we add guardrails, tests, traceability of every step and human approval for sensitive actions.

Do you build apps for both Android and iOS?

Yes. We usually start with Android in Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, then bring the app to iOS with Compose Multiplatform, which shares most of the code between both platforms.

Can you add AI to an existing app?

Yes: smart search, assistants, reading documents or photos, AI running directly on the phone. We start with an audit of the existing app to choose the right approach.

What does a mobile app security audit include?

Verification against the OWASP MASVS standard, tests following the MASTG guide, a MobSF analysis triaged by hand, and a penetration test if needed. You receive a report with each vulnerability ranked by severity, its evidence and the recommended fix, then we verify the fixes.

Does the AI understand Tunisian Arabic?

It is one of our specialities. Our EasyFind app understands French, Arabic and Tunisian Arabic written in Latin letters, with digits standing in for letters (3, 7, 9).

How much does a project cost, and how long does it take?

It depends on the scope. After a first conversation, you receive a written proposal with scope, schedule and price, before any commitment. To reduce risk, we often suggest starting with a limited prototype.

Where are you based?

ITBerg is a Tunisian company. For any project, in Tunisia or elsewhere, write to us at contact@itberg.tn. We answer in French, Arabic or English.
