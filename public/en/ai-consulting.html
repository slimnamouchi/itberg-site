How an engagement works

  1. First conversation

    You present your context and goals. We tell you honestly whether and how we can help.

  2. Proposal

    Scope, schedule and price in writing, before any commitment.

  3. Assessment

    Interviews with your teams, review of existing tools and data.

  4. Recommendations

    A prioritised roadmap, concrete actions and their estimated costs.

  5. Implementation and review

    We support you during implementation, then review the measured results.

The length of an engagement depends on its scope. It is agreed with you in the proposal.
