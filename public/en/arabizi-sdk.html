Arabizi SDK: your app finally understands Tunisian Arabic

“9adech el 7lib”, “nlawej 3la blombi”, “سوم الزيت”: your customers write the way they speak. Our SDK for searching and understanding Tunisian Arabic integrates into your web and mobile apps, so they find what they are looking for the first time.

The problem: your search does not speak Tunisian

Three scripts, one request

French, Arabic, and Derja typed in Latin letters with digits (3, 7, 9). A standard search understands only one of them.

No official spelling

“7lib”, “hlib”, “7leeb”: everyone writes their own way. One letter off, and the user finds nothing.

Customers lost in silence

When search answers “no results”, users assume the product does not exist, and go elsewhere.

What the SDK brings

Understands all three scripts

A query in Derja, Arabic or French leads to the same results, even when they are mixed in one sentence.

Tolerates typos and variants

Common spellings and typing mistakes no longer make search fail.

Fast and discreet

Built to answer quickly, even on an entry-level phone. Depending on your needs, it can run inside the app itself, without sending searches to a server.

Tailored to your domain

The vocabulary is prepared for your business: your products, your services, your customers’ words.

For which apps?

Any app where Tunisian customers type text. For example:

  • online shops and product catalogues;
  • directories of services, professionals or shops;
  • delivery, transport or booking apps;
  • online help, FAQs and customer service;
  • public-sector or non-profit apps meant for everyone.

How it works

  1. Your real searches

    You send us examples of what your customers type, as they write it, with no personal data.

  2. A measured trial

    We measure what the SDK finds on your own examples, before any commitment: you see a result, not a promise.

  3. Integration

    We help you integrate it into your web, Android or iOS app, or we integrate it for you.

  4. Follow-up

    The vocabulary grows with searches that returned nothing, and every improvement is measured before it ships.

Frequently asked questions

What is Arabizi?

It is Arabic, here Tunisian Derja, written in Latin letters, with digits for sounds that Latin letters lack: 3 for ع, 7 for ح, 9 for ق. For example “9adech el 7lib” for “how much is the milk”.

Are my customers’ searches sent to a server?

Not necessarily: depending on your app, the SDK can run directly on the device. If running on a server is a better fit, we tell you clearly what travels and where.

Which platforms can it be integrated into?

Web and mobile apps, Android and iOS. We look at your app together before proposing the best way to integrate it.

How much does it cost?

It depends on your app and your volume. After a first conversation and a trial on your examples, you receive a written proposal, before any commitment.

Can we try it before committing?

Yes: try our online demo, then send us examples of your customers’ searches. We measure the result on your data before you decide.

Do your customers write in Derja?

Send us about twenty typical searches from your customers, as they write them. We will show you what the SDK understands.
