The harness: how we guide AI

An AI model on its own is powerful but unpredictable. What makes it useful in a business is everything built around it. That is the submerged part of the iceberg, and it is our job.

  • SCOPELimited access: the agent only uses the tools and data you authorise.
  • GRAPHDefined steps: the flow is described in a LangGraph graph; the agent does not leave the planned path.
  • GUARDRAILSControls: input and output filters, business rules, limits on possible actions.
  • EVALUATIONTests before production: reference question sets measure quality before every release, then continuously.
  • TRACESTraceability: every step is logged and can be replayed and explained.
  • HUMANHuman approval: sensitive actions stop and wait for a person’s approval.

Use cases for businesses

Examples of what AI can do today. These are ideas, not client references.

Assistant on your documents

The need: your teams keep looking for the right procedure, price or clause.

What AI brings: an internal assistant that answers from your own documents and cites its sources.

Search that understands Tunisian

The need: your customers write “nheb na3ref el prix”, in French, in Arabic or in Latin letters.

What AI brings: search that understands all three ways of writing, as in our EasyFind app.

Request-handling agent

The need: requests pile up waiting to be read, sorted and prepared.

What AI brings: an agent that classifies, drafts a reply and submits it to your team before sending.

Document extraction

The need: invoices, purchase orders or forms are re-typed by hand.

What AI brings: automatic reading of the useful fields, with human review of doubtful cases.

File preparation

The need: a file requires gathering information from several tools.

What AI brings: an agent that collects, checks and assembles, then lets you approve.

Summaries

The need: long reports, minutes, tenders: there is no time to read everything.

What AI brings: faithful summaries that link back to the original text.
