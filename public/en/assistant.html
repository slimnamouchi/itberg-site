An assistant that cites its sources and can say “I don’t know”

Ask a question about our services. The assistant looks for the answer in the pages of this site and shows you the exact passage with a link to check it. Your question stays on your device.

Under the surface

Its guardrails, in plain sight

  1. It cites its sources. Every answer is a passage of this site, with the link to read it in full.
  2. It stays on topic. Weather, recipes, homework: it politely declines, without making things up.
  3. It says when it doesn’t know. If no passage is close enough, it says so and suggests the team.
  4. Its rules don’t change. “Ignore your instructions” doesn’t work: they are written in the code.
  5. A human, at any time. The “Talk to a human” link is always there, and can put your question in an email that you send yourself.

What happens when you ask a question

No large language model: four simple, predictable and verifiable steps. It is the same harness principle we build around our clients’ AI agents.

1. Read, on your device

Your browser splits the question into keywords: accents removed, filler words set aside, French, English, Arabic and some Tunisian derja recognised.

2. Check the guardrails

Before any search: attempts to change the rules, off-topic requests, requests to attack someone, personal data in the message. Each case has its answer, written in advance.

3. Search the site’s pages

At each release, a script splits the public pages into passages and builds an index. The assistant reads it once, then ranks passages by relevance (BM25), with a small lexicon linking “price”, “cost” and “9adech”.

4. Quote, or hand over

It shows the closest passage and its link. If the score is too low, it doesn’t answer and points you to the team. The details of each answer are under “Under the surface”.

Its limits, stated plainly

  • It doesn’t reason, it retrieves. An unusual wording can land on a nearby passage, hence the link to the source.
  • It only knows what is published on this site.
  • It gives no prices and no commitments: only a written proposal from the team commits ITBerg.

Check it yourself

  • Open the developer tools, Network tab: you will see a single file read, the site’s public index, and no request containing your question.
  • Turn off Wi-Fi once the assistant is open: it keeps answering.
  • Nothing is kept: reload the page and the conversation is gone.
