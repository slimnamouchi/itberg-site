AI and LLM security audit

Is your chatbot, assistant or AI agent in contact with customers or sensitive data? We look for ways to hijack it before someone else does, and help you limit the possible damage.

The risks we test

  • INJECTIONPrompt injection: direct in the conversation, or indirect through a document, an email or a web page.
  • LEAKInformation leakage: personal data, internal documents, system instructions.
  • AGENCYExcessive agency: an agent that sends, changes or deletes more than it should.
  • OUTPUTImproper output handling: AI output reused as is in a page, a query or a command.
  • RAGKnowledge base: poisoned documents, access rights ignored during retrieval.
  • COSTUnbounded consumption: looping or oversized requests that blow up the bill.

Our method

  1. Mapping

    What the AI reads, what it can do, who it answers, and what would be serious.

  2. Attack testing

    Manual scenarios tailored to your business, complemented by automated campaigns with open-source tools.

  3. Report and fixes

    Each flaw with its evidence, impact and fix: filtering, permissions, human approval, content separation.

  4. Regression tests

    The attacks found become automated tests, replayed whenever the model or instructions change.

Frequently asked questions

What is prompt injection?

It is booby-trapped text, in a message, a document or a web page read by the AI, that tries to hijack its instructions: make it reveal information, ignore its rules or trigger an action. It is the first risk in the OWASP Top 10 for LLM applications.

What exactly do you test?

Your assistant, chatbot or agent as deployed: its instructions, the documents it reads, the tools it can call and what it returns to the user. We follow the OWASP Top 10 for LLM applications and add tests specific to your business.

Can you guarantee no attack will work?

No, and nobody honestly can: a language model is never 100% reliable. The audit reduces the risk and, above all, checks that the consequences of a hijack stay limited: least privilege, human approval, logging.

Do you need access to the model?

No. We test from the outside, like a user or an attacker, preferably on a test environment. Access to the system instructions and architecture allows a more thorough audit. We only work with your written authorisation.
