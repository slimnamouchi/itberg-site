03 · Security

Security for apps and AI

What an attacker would see, we see first: on your mobile apps, your APIs, and now your AI assistants and agents.

Mobile security audit

Verification against OWASP MASVS, tests following the MASTG guide, MobSF analysis and manual review.

  • MASVS
  • MASTG
  • MobSF

Mobile penetration testing

Network traffic, APIs, local storage, bypasses, reverse engineering, with written authorisation.

  • Pentest
  • Reverse engineering
